# YouTube Notes Chrome Extension

### The Simplest Way to Save Your Thoughts on YouTube Videos!

Ever wished you could capture your thoughts on YouTube? **YouTube Notes** lets you easily save thoughts and notes while watching videos, making it simple to revisit key takeaways anytime.

---

## Features

- **Quick Notes**: Capture your thoughts instantly while watching YouTube videos.
- **Easy Access**: View and manage your saved notes directly from the extension.
- **Boost Your Learning**: Never forget those key moments of insight again by saving them for later.

---

## How to Install

To get started with **YouTube Notes**, follow these simple steps:

1. Open **Google Chrome**.
2. Click on the three vertical dots in the upper right corner of Chrome.
3. Go to **More Tools** → **Extensions**.
4. In the top right corner of the Extensions page, enable **Developer Mode**.
5. Click **Load unpacked**.
6. Select the folder containing the extension files (e.g., `youtube-notes-v1.0`).
7. The extension should now be installed and visible in your extensions toolbar!

---

## How to Use

1. Once installed, open any YouTube video.
2. To open the **editor** and start taking notes, press **Alt + S** on your keyboard.
3. Type your notes in the editor window.
4. To view your saved thoughts, press **Alt + V** on your keyboard.
5. Your notes will be saved and linked to the specific video you’re watching for easy access later.
